ReImaGin — What the Model Asks the Image Generator to Draw

Paper: Reasoning with Image Generation — Nishad Singhi, Hector Garcia Rodriguez, Aditya Arora, Marcus Rohrbach & Anna Rohrbach (TU Darmstadt · hessian.AI), COLM 2026, arXiv:2609.16409.

📄 Paper Summary

ReImaGin gives a multimodal LLM (Gemini-3.1-Pro, GPT-5 or Qwen-3.5-27B) one free-form tool, generate_image(prompt, images). It calls an image generator (mainly Nano-Banana-Pro) in the middle of its reasoning to draw a helper picture, such as a line, a depth map or a floor plan, and then reads that picture to answer. It is training-free, and across six visual reasoning tasks (including Spatial457 collision prediction and MMSI multi-view spatial reasoning) it beats both text-only reasoning and the fixed-tool baseline Visual Sketchpad, by up to 25 points on path tracing.

🖊 My Key Notes

The instruction is not "draw what happens next". It is "draw a helper mark on the current image". The MLLM writes the instruction itself, following a strategy and one worked example in its prompt.

TaskInstruction sent to the image generator
Collision (handcrafted)"Add a line through the yellow bus, in the direction the bus is currently facing, extending to both the front and the back."
Collision (discovered)"Draw a bright red arrow starting from the front bumper of the green SUV and extending straight forward along its current heading to show its path."
Occlusion counting"Remove the black square from the image to complete the pattern of chairs."
Path tracing"Convert the dashed lines into solid lines."
Depth"Create a depth map of this image" (discovered: "high-contrast grayscale depth map")
Multi-view roomStep 1: "Combine the two images to create a unified view". Step 2: "Create a top-down blueprint / map of this scene"
Puzzle"Remove the white space from this image", then subtract the original image to get the missing piece
